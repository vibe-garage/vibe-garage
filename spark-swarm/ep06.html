Episode 5 gave your crabs rhythm: waves, a break to breathe in, a banner that calls each one. But a crab that reaches the spark just hugs it — and a game where nothing can hurt you has no tension, because no mistake ever costs anything. This episode gives the swarm teeth: health for the spark, ten health per touch, and the fix for the hug that lands sixty hits in one second.

What you build

  1. Give the spark health: a health_changed signal, Max Health 100 set in _ready, and take_damage — subtract, clamp at zero, and shout the new number on the signal.
  2. Show the hit: a red flash. The sprite blinks red and settles back over a quarter of a second on a tween. No hit sound — the sound is saved for a later episode.
  3. Let the crab call it: the crab gets Damage 10 and a touch check after move_and_slide. When a slide collision's other body is the spark, the crab calls take_damage.
  4. Watch one hug empty the bar: a slide collision is a report, not an event. A crab that stays touching re-fires the check every physics step — 10 × 60 = 600 damage a second, and 100 health is gone in ten frames.
  5. Add the mercy rule: a 0.5-second hit cooldown on the spark. take_damage returns false while it runs and true when a hit lands, so at most two hits a second (20 damage) get through — and the whole swarm shares that one speed limit.
  6. Knock back on a landed hit only: because take_damage reports whether the hit counted, the crab bounces off only when it actually hurt you.
  7. Run the waves and count: waves 1 to 3 with hits landing — 100, then 90, then 70, then 40 — and the breaks turn into health-counting time.

One touch, one hit (spark.gd)

signal health_changed(new_health: int)

## Health the spark starts with. Ten clean crab hits empty it.
@export var max_health: int = 100
## Seconds after a landed hit during which the spark can't be hurt
## again. This cooldown belongs to the spark, not to any one crab,
## so a whole swarm shares the same speed limit.
@export var hit_cooldown: float = 0.5

var health: int
var _hit_left: float = 0.0


func _ready() -> void:
	health = max_health


## Something hurt the spark. Returns true when the hit counted, so
## the attacker knows it connected (the crab bounces off only then).
## While the mercy cooldown is still running, the hit is ignored:
## a touch that stays touching does not keep paying out.
func take_damage(amount: int) -> bool:
	if _hit_left > 0.0:
		return false
	_hit_left = hit_cooldown
	health = clampi(health - amount, 0, max_health)
	health_changed.emit(health)
	_flash_damage()
	return true

What's in the folder

  • spark.gd: the movement and zap from Episodes 1–5, plus health, take_damage and the damage flash (new in this episode)
  • crab.gd: the chase, plus Damage 10, the slide-collision touch check and the knockback (new in this episode)
  • wave_spawner.gd and wave_banner.gd: Episode 5's wave system, unchanged
  • arena.tscn, spark.tscn and crab.tscn: the scenes, with the spark's new health settings in the Inspector

Run it

  1. Install Godot 4.8 beta 1 (the standard build).
  2. Unzip ep06.zip.
  3. In Godot's Project Manager, click Import and pick project.godot inside the spark-swarm-ep06 folder.
  4. Press Play (F5). Move with WASD, the arrow keys or a gamepad. Zap with Space or the bottom face button.

One honest limit: health can sit at zero and nothing happens. The spark keeps zapping at 0 — there is no game over yet. Ending the run is Episode 7's job. The bonk is silent, too: the hit sound is saved for a later episode.

Set crab damage to 20 and predict the run before you play: how many hits empty a full bar? (Five: 100 ÷ 20.) Then decide what a hit should cost in your game, and set your own numbers.
